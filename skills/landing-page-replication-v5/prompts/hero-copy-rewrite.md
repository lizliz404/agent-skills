# Hero / product-copy rewrite

Text hits uncanny valley before pixel drift. Use this after structure exists, before ±2px polish.

## Hard bans (English SaaS 2020–2024 burn list)

**Names / tokens:** Nexus, Flow, Sync, Loop, Hive, Prism, Orbit, Quantum, Synergy, Pulse (as product name), Apex, Zenith

**Sentence molds:**
- "The X platform for Y"
- "Where teams do their best work"
- "Built for how modern teams work"
- "Unlock the power of …"
- "AI-powered everything" with no concrete object

## Prefer

| Weak | Stronger |
|------|----------|
| "Product Name" | Specific verb + object: "Ship your quarterly board report by Tuesday" |
| "up to 30% saved" | "$12,480 recovered" |
| "Acme Corp" / lorem | Odd but credible team: "Priya's team at Threshold" |
| Round metrics 100/90/80 | Messy: 87.3%, 71%, 68.9% |

## Process

1. List every visible string in hero + primary mock.
2. Strike anything on the ban list or mold list.
3. Rewrite for the **replica's** product truth (study clone: anonymized plausible CRM/eng data; ship product: real claims only).
4. Never paste target marketing headlines verbatim (IP + uncanny).
5. Re-run Generic-SaaS sniff (cover wordmark).

## Study vs ship

- **Study:** Plausible fiction OK; still avoid ban-list names.
- **Ship:** Every metric must be defensible; legal review for comparative claims.
